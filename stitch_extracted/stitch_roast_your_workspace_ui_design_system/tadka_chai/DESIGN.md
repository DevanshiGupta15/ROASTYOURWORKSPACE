## Brand & Style

This design system sets the visual benchmark for workplace banter, catharsis, and culturally rich humor within domain-verified corporate walls. It balances the spice, theatrical irreverence, and vibrant wit of desi pop-culture with enterprise-grade hygiene, safety, and polish.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Punchy & Irreverent:** Snappy, high-tempo banter that calls out the everyday absurdities of sprint planning, appraisal cycles, standup monologues, and cold cafeteria samosas.
- **Vibrant & Culturally Grounded:** Infused with dynamic street-food spice, vibrant transit typography energy, and modern Bollywood satire without tipping into retro kitsch.
- **Psychologically Safe & Clean:** Maintains a sharp distinction between harmless satire and toxic harassment. UI elements project confidentiality, real-time safety guardrails, and cryptographic anonymity.

### Design Movement: High-Contrast Contemporary Satire
The aesthetic synthesizes modern **High-Contrast Digital Grotesque** with tactile, spicy accents:
- **Crisp Structural Discipline:** Sharp semantic hierarchy, strict layout grids, and ultra-readable surfaces keep the platform looking like high-tier SaaS rather than a reckless forum.
- **Vibrant Pop Energy:** Electric saffron heat glows, warm turmeric badges, and electric purple punch-tags pop against deep corporate midnight slate.
- **Dynamic Tactile Reactions:** Reaction bars, meme attachments, and streak counters feel responsive, physical, and satisfying to tap.

## Colors

The palette establishes an evening "after-hours chai tapri" ambiance in default dark mode, paired with spicy thermal accents that signal high-engagement roasts.

### Key Semantic Colors
- **Primary — Chili Saffron (`#FF4500` / `#FF5A1F`):** Represents high-temperature roasts, active hot-takes, upvotes, and primary calls to banter.
- **Secondary — Masala Gold / Turmeric (`#F59E0B`):** Applied to user streaks, "Hall of Flame" awards, trending tags, and standout employee roasts.
- **Tertiary — Bollywood Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`):** Dedicated to meme categories, Bollywood trope tags, media badge ribbons, and flair chips.
- **Neutral — Midnight Obsidian (`#0F172A` canvas, `#1E293B` card surfaces, `#334155` elevated containers):** A deep, corporate-friendly slate that elevates content legibility and eliminates eye strain during late-night venting.

### System & State Colors
- **Domain Verified Guard — Emerald Mint (`#10B981`):** Signals verified corporate or institutional domains (`@company.com`, `@institute.edu.in`), verified company badges, and safe status markers.
- **Decency Intercept & Guardrail — Crimson Red (`#EF4444`):** Real-time NLP decency engine warnings, toxicity flags, blocked profanities, and instant report triggers.
- **Border Subtleties (`#334155` & `#1E293B`):** Crisp structural dividers that prevent elements from bleeding into dark backgrounds.

## Typography

The type system blends the geometry of **Outfit** for headlines, the readability of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for multi-line workplace roasts, and the precision of **JetBrains Mono** for domain tags, metadata, and anonymity hash keys.

- **Display & Headings (Outfit):** Tight tracking, extra-bold cuts, and high-impact vertical density ensure memes and roast hooks punch through the feed immediately.
- **Narrative & Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted for rapid scanning of paragraph-length corporate rants, gossip threads, and poll descriptions with tall x-height and generous line height.
- **Technical & Identity Metadata (JetBrains Mono):** Used exclusively for domain pills (`@swiggy.in`, `@flipkart.com`), masked avatar identities (`#IncognitoBiryani`), time stamps, decency engine strike alerts, and reaction counts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout centers on focused, feed-first consumption optimized for rapid mobile scrolling and wide desktop split-views.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Feed Constraint:** Central feed columns are pinned to a max-width of `680px` on desktop to preserve optimal reading line lengths for witty text bursts and vertical meme consumption.
- **Desktop Dashboard Layout:** Three-column view featuring Left Navigation & Domain Leaderboard (`260px`), Center Primary Feed (`680px`), and Right Trending Memes / Spice Meter panel (`340px`).
- **Mobile Grid:** Single fluid column bound by `margin: 1rem` safe gutters, prioritizing high information density with edge-to-edge interactive reaction bars.

### Rhythmic Scale
Spacing uses a base 4px/8px modular cadence:
- `space-xs (4px)` for badge inner padding, reaction emoji spacing, and dot-dividers.
- `space-sm (8px)` for action button grouping, icon-to-label gaps, and chip lists.
- `space-md (16px)` for card body padding, comment tier indents, and drawer elements.
- `space-lg (24px)` for vertical separations between discrete feed post cards.
- `space-xl (36px)` for modal padding, section header divides, and empty-state placeholders.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal container layering** coupled with **tactile thermal glow outlines** rather than heavy muddy drop-shadows.

### Layer Hierarchy
1. **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep Midnight Obsidian (`#0F172A`).
2. **Layer 1 (Card & Post Surfaces):** Midnight Slate (`#1E293B`) framed by a subtle, crisp border (`#334155` at 60% opacity).
3. **Layer 2 (Floating Triggers, Meme Attachment Trays, Reaction Bars):** Lifted Slate (`#334155`) with a 1px border (`#475569`).
4. **Layer 3 (Modals, Decency Warning Drawers, Pinned Alerts):** Deep Obsidian with 80% opacity and `16px` backdrop-blur (`blur(16px)`), suspended over the feed.

### Thematic Glows & Accents
- **Fiery Post Elevation:** High-engagement roasts (trending > 500 roasts) inherit an ambient aura: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 69, 0, 0.25)`.
- **Masala Hall-of-Fame Elevation:** Weekly winner cards utilize an amber rim: `0 0 20px -2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs `roundedness: 2`, delivering a balanced 12px (`0.75rem`) to 16px (`1rem`) geometry that feels modern, accessible, and friendly.

- **Primary Cards & Modals:** Standardized to `rounded-lg` (`16px`), softening edge geometry while keeping content bounded.
- **Reaction Pills & Badges:** Use full pills (`9999px`) to maintain conversational, tap-friendly affordances across mobile touch surfaces.
- **Image & Meme Attachments:** Clipped strictly to `rounded-md` (`10px`) with 1px inset slate borders to integrate third-party meme templates seamlessly.
- **Interactive Buttons:** Uniform `rounded-md` (`8px`) on functional utilities and full pill (`9999px`) on floating primary triggers.

## Components

### 1. Anonymous Feed Roast Cards
- **Header:** Features a procedural masked anonymous avatar (e.g., `#SamosaBandit`, `#AgileDosa`), paired with a verified domain pill (`@swiggy.in` in `#10B981` mint tint) and a monospaced relative timestamp (`32m ago`).
- **Body:** Multi-line text set in `body-lg`, auto-linking hashtags like `#WFHScams` and department tags (`Engineering`, `HR`, `Leadership`).
- **Meme Media Container:** Inset box with rounded borders, lazy-loaded with a dark placeholder shimmer and watermark stamp.

### 2. Interactive Reaction & Roast Bars
- Segmented pills anchored to the footer of each card:
  - **Roast (🔥):** Increments primary counter with a saffron burst transition.
  - **Laugh (😂):** Highlights with Masala Amber illumination.
  - **Salty (🧂):** Secondary subtle feedback with slate highlight.
  - **Report / Decency Flag (🚩):** Discretely positioned at the trailing edge; turns crimson on click.

### 3. Real-Time Decency Alert Banner (Auto-Intercept)
- Appears inline inside the draft creation composer when toxic or non-consensual personal identifiers are flagged by the decency engine.
- Styled with an alert crimson background (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`), border `1px solid #EF4444`, and monospace copy (`"Decency Engine Intercept: Remove real names or PII to keep the roast workplace-safe"`).

### 4. Verified Domain Badges & Pills
- Pill containers with a soft emerald background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`), solid emerald text (`#10B981`), and a tiny shielded checkmark icon. Displays only authenticated corporate email suffixes (`@tcs.com`, `@zomato.com`).

### 5. Indian Meme Drawer & Template Picker
- Bottom sheet or slide-over drawer populated with curated, search-indexed Bollywood, regional, and cricket meme templates (e.g., Vasooli Bhai, Hera Pheri, Shark Tank India punchlines).
- Categorized via chips using the tertiary purple palette (`#8B5CF6`).

### 6. Buttons & Inputs
- **Primary Button:** Fiery Saffron (`#FF4500`) fill with dark obsidian text, bold tracking, and a bright hover state.
- **Secondary Button:** Slate surface (`#334155`) with high-contrast white text and subtle 1px border.
- **Input Fields:** Recessed dark background (`#0B1120`), 1px slate border (`#334155`), transitioning to a Saffron ring on focus (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #FF4500`).
